title: Filter classes and maintenance

tags: filter, G4, F7, ePM1, maintenance, pressure drop

---
G4 (coarse) filters protect equipment from dust and insects. F7 / ePM1 60% filters are needed for offices and anything with people in it.

Replace filters when the pressure drop doubles compared to a clean filter, or at least every 3-6 months. A clogged filter is the most common cause of "low airflow" claims.

Spare filters are sold by model: the code is FL-<unit code>-<class>, for example FL-NW-ID-200-F7.
