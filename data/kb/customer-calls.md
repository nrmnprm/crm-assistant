---
title: Logging customer calls
type: how-to
tags: call, log, timeline, next step
updated: 2026-03-22
---
After every call with a customer, add a note to the customer timeline: what was discussed, what was promised and the next step with a date. Calls through the office phone are attached automatically; you only add the note and the next step.

A next step without a date is not a next step: the CRM will not remind you about it.
