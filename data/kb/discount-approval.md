---
title: Discount approval matrix
type: policy
tags: discount, approval, pricing, margin
updated: 2026-06-20
---
Sales managers may give up to 5% off the list price on their own.

From 5% to 12% the discount needs the approval of the head of sales: create an approval request from the quote ("Request discount") and explain the reason (competitor offer, volume, strategic customer).

Above 12% only the sales director can approve, and only for project deals with a signed specification.

Discounts never stack with the volume price list. Spare parts and commissioning services are not discounted.
