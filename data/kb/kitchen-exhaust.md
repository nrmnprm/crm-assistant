---
title: Kitchen exhaust requirements
type: product guide
tags: kitchen, exhaust, grease, temperature, restaurant
updated: 2026-05-30
---
Commercial kitchen exhaust needs fans rated for at least 120 °C continuous air temperature and a grease filter in the hood.

Only roof fans of the NW-RF series (rated for 120 °C) are approved for kitchen exhaust. Axial wall fans and inline duct fans are not suitable.

Airflow for a kitchen hood: about 1,000-1,500 m³/h per metre of hood length.
