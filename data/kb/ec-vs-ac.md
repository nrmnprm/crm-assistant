---
title: EC vs AC motors
type: product guide
tags: EC, AC, motor, efficiency, speed control
updated: 2026-03-03
---
EC (electronically commutated) motors are 25-40% more efficient than AC motors, have built-in speed control (0-10 V or Modbus) and run quieter at partial speed. They cost 30-60% more.

AC motors need an external speed controller or a frequency converter for speed control. They are simpler to repair and cheaper to buy.

Recommend EC when the fan runs more than 8 hours a day, when speed control is needed, or when noise matters. For short daily operation AC is usually the better buy.
