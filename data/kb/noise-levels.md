---
title: Noise levels: what is acceptable where
type: product guide
tags: noise, dB(A), sound, quiet
updated: 2026-04-18
---
Catalogue noise values are sound pressure levels in dB(A) at 3 m in free field.

Typical limits: bedrooms and hotel rooms 30-35 dB(A), offices and meeting rooms 35-40, restaurants and shops 45-50, workshops 60-70.

Every 10 dB(A) sounds twice as loud. EC fans at reduced speed are 5-8 dB(A) quieter than at full speed, which is why oversized EC fans running slowly can be a good choice for quiet rooms.

Duct silencers reduce noise by 10-25 dB(A) and add 20-50 Pa of pressure loss; include that in the duty point.
