title: Lead qualification checklist
type: how-to
tags: lead, qualification, new customer, discovery
updated: 2026-04-27
---
Before quoting a new lead, find out:
- the object type and rooms (office, restaurant, warehouse, flat);
- the airflow or the room sizes, and what is installed now;
- who decides and who pays (contractor, end customer, designer);
- the deadline and the budget;
- whether a project specification exists.

Log the answers in the lead card. A lead without object type and deadline is not passed to a quote.
