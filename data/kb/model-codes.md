NW-AX — axial wall fans, the number is the impeller diameter in mm (NW-AX-400).
NW-ID — circular inline duct fans, the number is the duct diameter (NW-ID-200 fits a 200 mm duct).
NW-RD — rectangular duct fans, the numbers are the duct size in cm (NW-RD-60-30).
NW-RF — roof fans.
The suffix E means an EC motor (NW-ID-200E). All NW-RF roof fans are rated for 120 °C air.
