title: Returns and warranty policy
type: policy
tags: returns, warranty, RMA, refund
updated: 2026-08-14
---
All fans and air handling units sold by Northwind Air carry a 24-month manufacturer warranty from the date of delivery. Filters, belts and other consumables are not covered.

Stock items can be returned within 14 days of delivery if they are unused and in the original packaging. A 15% restocking fee applies; the fee is waived when the return is caused by our error (wrong model shipped, damaged in transit).

Made-to-order units (anything with the status "on order" at the time of the sale, custom voltages, special coatings) cannot be returned. Warranty still applies to them.

A warranty claim is accepted only with the serial number, the invoice number and photos of the nameplate and of the fault. Units that were installed without a motor protection device or outside the allowed air temperature range lose their warranty.

Refunds are paid to the original payment method within 10 business days after the returned unit passes inspection at the warehouse.
