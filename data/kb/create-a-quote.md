---
title: How to create a quote in the CRM
type: how-to
tags: quote, proposal, offer, CRM
updated: 2026-05-11
---
1. Open the customer card and press "New quote". If the customer is new, create the customer first: a quote cannot exist without one.
2. Add items from the catalogue by model code (for example NW-ID-200) or with the selection wizard. The price and availability come from the catalogue and cannot be typed by hand.
3. Set the discount. Anything above 5% sends an approval request automatically (see the discount approval matrix).
4. Choose delivery: pickup, courier or pallet. Free delivery is applied automatically above $1,500.
5. Press "Send to customer". The CRM generates a PDF with the validity period (14 days by default) and logs the e-mail on the customer timeline.

A quote does not reserve stock. To hold items for a customer, convert the quote into an order or create a reservation (see "Stock reservations").
