---
title: Stock reservations
type: how-to
tags: stock, reservation, reserve, availability
updated: 2026-04-02
---
A reservation holds stock items for a customer for 3 business days. Open the model card, press "Reserve", choose the customer and the quantity.

Only confirmed orders hold stock longer. When a reservation expires, the items return to free stock automatically and the manager gets a notification.

In-transit items can be reserved too; the reservation then starts on the day the shipment arrives.
