title: Spare parts and lead times
type: policy
tags: spare parts, motor, impeller, lead time
updated: 2026-02-10
---
Motors, impellers and capacitors for current models are stocked for 7 years after the model is discontinued.

Spare parts in stock ship in 1-2 days. Parts that are not in stock take 3-5 weeks. Spare parts are not discounted.
