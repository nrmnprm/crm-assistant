---
title: How to register a warranty claim (RMA) in the CRM
type: how-to
tags: warranty, RMA, claim, complaint, defect
updated: 2026-07-02
---
1. Open the customer card and go to the "Claims" tab, then press "New claim".
2. Choose the order the unit came from. The serial number field is mandatory; the CRM checks that the serial belongs to that order.
3. Attach photos of the nameplate and of the fault, and the customer's description in their own words.
4. Pick the claim type: "Does not start", "Noise or vibration", "Low airflow", "Damaged on delivery" or "Other".
5. Save. The claim gets a number like RMA-2026-0153 and is routed to the service engineer on duty.

The engineer has 2 business days to give the customer a first answer. Do not promise a replacement before the engineer's decision: most "low airflow" claims turn out to be a wrong duty point or a clogged filter.
