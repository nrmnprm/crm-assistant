In stock: shipped the next business day if the order is confirmed before 14:00, delivered in 1-2 business days within the region.

In transit: the ETA is shown in the CRM on the model card and is usually 5-12 business days. Do not quote a date that is earlier than the ETA in the CRM.

On order: 4-6 weeks from the moment the prepayment is received. Custom voltages and coatings add 2 weeks.

Delivery is free for orders above $1,500 before tax; below that the courier rate from the price list applies. Pallet deliveries to construction sites need a contact person and a crane or forklift on site, which the customer confirms in writing.
