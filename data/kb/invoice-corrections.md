---
title: Invoice corrections and credit notes
type: how-to
tags: invoice, correction, credit note, refund
updated: 2026-02-27
---
Never edit a sent invoice. If the amount, the items or the customer details are wrong, issue a credit note for the full invoice and a new corrected invoice.

In the CRM: open the invoice, press "Credit note", choose "Full" or "Partial" and the reason. Finance approves credit notes above $1,000.
