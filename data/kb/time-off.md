---
title: Vacation and day-off requests
type: how-to
tags: vacation, day off, sick leave, request
updated: 2026-02-02
---
Request vacation at least 2 weeks ahead: in the CRM go to Profile → Requests → New request, choose the type and the dates. Your manager approves it.

Sick leave: tell your manager by phone in the morning and create the request on the day you are back, with the certificate attached.
