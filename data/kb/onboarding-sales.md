Day 1: accounts, CRM access, phone. Read the discount, payment and returns policies.
Day 2: product training — model codes, fan selection basics, EC vs AC.
Day 3: shadow a senior manager on calls.
Day 4: create test quotes in the CRM training space.
Day 5: first own leads, with a senior manager reviewing every quote for two weeks.
