---
title: Payment terms and credit limits
type: policy
tags: payment, credit limit, prepayment, invoice, overdue
updated: 2026-03-15
---
New customers pay 100% in advance for the first three orders.

After three paid orders a customer can get net-30 terms with a credit limit set by the finance department (usually $5,000-$20,000). The limit and the current balance are on the customer card, visible to sales only.

When an invoice is more than 30 days overdue, the customer goes on credit hold: new orders need prepayment until the debt is paid. Sales managers get a daily list of overdue invoices in their briefing.
