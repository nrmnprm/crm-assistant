---
title: Installation checklist for inline duct fans
type: product guide
tags: installation, inline, duct, checklist, vibration
updated: 2026-07-21
---
1. Install with the airflow arrow in the direction of flow.
2. Leave at least 1 duct diameter of straight duct before and 1.5 after the fan.
3. Use flexible connectors on both sides to stop vibration travelling along the duct.
4. Fit a motor protection device; without it the warranty is void.
5. Keep access for service: the cover must open without removing the fan.
6. Check the current draw after start-up; it must not exceed the nameplate value.
